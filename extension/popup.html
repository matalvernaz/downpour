Downpour

Checking whether Downpour is running.

The browser does not let Downpour into private windows yet. To capture there, also allow it in the browser's settings for this extension: "Allow in Incognito" in Chrome, "Allow in InPrivate" in Edge, "Run in Private Windows" in Firefox.

Downpour cannot capture downloads until it may read all websites. It needs that to see the cookies and form submissions that come with a download.
