# Downpour browser extension: privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The Downpour extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox connects your browser to
Downpour, a download manager that runs on your own Windows computer.

## What the extension handles, and why

- **Download details:** the address of a download and where it redirects
  to, its file name, size and type, the page it came from, and your
  browser's user agent. Downpour needs these to download the same file.
- **Cookies for that address:** many sites only serve a file to signed-in
  visitors. Downpour receives the cookies your browser would send for the
  download's address and uses them only in its own requests to that site.
- **Links on a page, with their text:** only when you choose to download a
  selection or all the links on a page.
- **Settings and a diagnostic log:** your capture settings and a log of
  what the extension did, kept in the browser's storage for the extension.
  The log records addresses with their secret parts removed and never
  records cookies.

## Where it goes

Only to the Downpour app on the same computer, through the browser's
native messaging. The extension has no server and contacts none; the
developer receives nothing.

Downpour uses this information to download files from the sites they come
from. It keeps its download list on your computer, with cookies and
passwords encrypted by Windows, and writes logs on your computer with
cookies, passwords and link tokens removed.

## What it never does

No analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data, no transfer
to anyone else, and no use for anything other than downloading what you
asked for. Downpour's use of this data follows the Chrome Web Store User
Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Your control

Turn capture off, for one site or entirely, in the extension's panel.
Remove the extension or Downpour at any time. Downpour's own data is in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Downpour` on your computer, and you can delete it.

## Contact

Use the contact email on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
